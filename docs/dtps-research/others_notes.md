# DTPS research notes (wiki-sourced, 2026-10-24)

Method: raw wikitext of every page was downloaded (`?action=raw`) and read directly, so infobox values below are the literal template parameters, not a summary. Raw copies sit in `..\dtps_raw\`. Quotes are verbatim from the wiki. "Not on the wiki" means the infobox parameter is blank and no prose number exists.

Conventions used in the JSON files (aligned with the other files already in this folder): `share` = fraction of attack cycles in which the row OCCURS, avoidable rows included (they are flagged `avoidable: true` and never counted); all shares of a file sum to <= 1. Exceptions, each stated in the file's `conventions` field: a row that is a mutually exclusive alternative to another row (Ocellus's spread void shot vs the melee auto; 'Embrace... annihilation...' vs 'Death... awaits...') or that is simultaneous with the others (the Maggot King's Roaring rows all fire in one screech event) carries `share: 0` with its occurrence share in `shareIfCounted`. `maxHit: null` = the wiki gives no number (do not read as 0). `uncertain: true` on a row or file = a wiki gap, explained in `note` / `uncertainReason`.

## Part A - boss models

### Ocellus (The Fractured Archive, final boss; released 20 October 2026)
Files: `ocellus__phase_1.json`, `ocellus__phase_2.json`, `ocellus__phase_3.json`

1. Infobox: `|attack speed1 = 5`, `|attack speed2 = 0`, `|attack speed3 = 6`; `|hitpoints1 = 2500`, `|hitpoints2 =` BLANK, `|hitpoints3 = 5250`; `|max hit =` BLANK; `|attack style =` BLANK; combat 1121 / (blank) / 1601; Demon attribute (phases 1 and 3); size 3; immune to cannons; 100% poison resistance.
2. HP table: team size 1 and 2 -> `Phase 1 2,500 | Remainder 5,250`; team 8 -> 8,880 | 18,640. Phase 2 has NO wiki hitpoints: players hit The Blade, whose stored damage is 'effectively transferring half of all damage dealt to the boss's phase 3 health bar'. The sheet's 2500 for phase 2 was kept and flagged.
3. Phase 1 autos: 'attacking with ranged (orange) and magic (blue) projectiles' - no max hit, no ratio, no prayer statement anywhere. The only quantified phase-1 damage: tendril slam 'up to 35' (2-tile AoE, dodgeable), charged attack 'Death... awaits...' 17-18 (one bolt, twice per phase, if enough tendrils were stood on) vs 'Embrace... annihilation...' '80+ damage in total' (failure branch).
4. Phase 2: all positional ('Standing at the intersection of the orange portals will avoid damage', purple pools, orange portal hop; 'repeat 3 times'); nothing quantified -> expected DTPS 0.
5. Phase 3 (quadrants 1-3): rotation '2 standard melee attacks followed by a directional wave attack ... After 2 wave attacks, he will begin a charged blade attack' (7 events). Melee autos are prayed (no max hit on the wiki); the smoke wave is dodged; the charged blade is a flick (prayer off then Protect from Melee back on; 1-3 spins by quadrant). 'If he is unable to reach the targeted player, he will attack with a powerful spread void shot dealing 20+ damage to each player' - this becomes the auto for a non-melee solo loadout.
6. Phase 3 specials: Void Vine 'upwards of 50 damage' if a teammate does not break it (solo behaviour unknown); Void Rifts/Augmenters/Siphons (44 HP) 'Failure to kill them will damage all players'; Insolence 3 wide waves; Untether eruption '40+' + landing slam 'a similar amount' (step under, step out on the final glow) with magic autos meanwhile ('pray mage'); Obliteration kills if you do not jump quadrants.
7. Final quadrant ('Phase 4', 'End...'): 8-step cycle: Deflect Melee + rise (+Void Rifts) -> half-current-HP steal (returned by an orb) -> 6 void explosions every 2 ticks (dodge by moving; boss takes 60% less damage) -> land -> Soul Split + void blast of a random style ('40+ damage; if prayed, it will deal ~6-7 damage') -> void floor + Deflect Range -> steal + explosions -> blast; repeats.
8. The ONLY quantified unavoidable damage under standard play: the prayed void blasts (~6-7, twice per enrage cycle), the recoverable half-HP steals, and 2 x 17-18 in phase 1. Everything else is prayed (max unknown) or dodged.
9. Doubts: (a) no max hit for any auto (ranged/magic P1, melee P3, smite) - the sheet cannot compute prayer leaks without an in-game number; (b) the 25%-per-quadrant HP split and the ~60-tick enrage cycle are my estimates; (c) whether prayers reduce the 17-18 bolt and the 20+ void shot is unstated; (d) the Ocellus page and the strategies disagree on the sign of tendril damage ('take some damage but will be healed' vs 'slightly heal you').
10. `Ocellus/Strategies` does not exist (HTTP 404); the guide is `The Fractured Archive/Strategies#Ocellus` (search confirmed no other guide).

### Maggot King (Vampyrium, solo instance; released 30 June 2026)
Files: `maggot_king__far.json`, `maggot_king__nearby.json`, `maggot_king__roaring.json`

1. Infobox: `|hitpoints = 1500`, `|combat = 741`, `|size = 5`, `|attack speed = varies`, `|attack style = Ranged, Magic, Melee`, `|max hit = 46 (Magic / Ranged),~55 (Melee lunge)`, aggressive Yes, poisonous Yes, Fire weakness.
2. What the three versions are: the infobox differs only in `|def1 = 200 |def2 = 200 |def3 = 100`, `|dmagic1 = 150 |dmagic2 = 400 |dmagic3 = 150`, `|elementalweaknesspercent1 = 80 |elementalweaknesspercent2 = 5 |elementalweaknesspercent3 = 80`. Matched to the prose: FAR = the ranged/magic spit phase (player across the arena from the boss), NEARBY = the melee-slam phase at the bone wall ('magic defence will be temporarily increased from +150 to +400'), ROARING = the venator screech ('its Defence level is temporarily halved to 100'). The wiki never states the mapping explicitly.
3. Far: green ranged spit and yellow magic spit 'can deal up to 46 damage, and are fully blocked by prayer'; style fixed per segment (ranged first, flips at each screech) so the prayer is camped, not flicked. Chip: sticky bile '2-4 damage' (disease hitsplat, stat drain, 1-tick attack delay), poisonous bile 'minor damage' + 45 s poison-immunity drain; the ranged spit poisons 'starting at 8 damage' (through-prayer unclear).
4. Nearby: head slam 3x4 'up to 55 damage (reduced to 35 with Protect from Melee)' = prayer factor 35/55 = 0.636 ('~40%' in the prose). Standard play dodges every slam ('moving towards its easternmost or westernmost tiles when it is about to attack') and punishes it; three biles launched per slam -> bile chip.
5. Roaring: 'turn off their protection prayer prior to this, otherwise they will take 15-25 damage per tick; players may take damage up to three times'; carrion piles 'up to 25' (shadow-telegraphed), small bits 'up to 10' ('it may be more beneficial to simply tank them'); a larva per screech from ~83% HP (3 dmg +1/tick leech if it reaches you; killed mid-air with a standard bow).
6. Rotation: 'always start with ranged attacks. It will use ranged/magic 4-8 times before using a screech'; melee from '~980 (~65%)'; melee-only from '~305 (~20%)'; multiple larvae from '~299 (~19%)'; 'attack speed will increase by 1 tick after the first screech'.
7. Expected DTPS under standard play: near zero - bile chip (2-4), a small carrion bit (<=10) in roughly half the screeches, a late prayer-off tick (15-25) now and then. The wiki agrees: 'Perfect kills will require minimal sustain, as the boss deals very little chip damage'.
8. Doubts: (a) NO attack speed in ticks anywhere - `sheetSpeedTicks` is 0 in all three files and must be measured; (b) 'increase by 1 tick' is ambiguous (faster or a longer interval?); (c) chip shares (0.10 / 0.15 bile, 0.5 small carrion) are my estimates; (d) melee sub-style unstated; (e) poison through a blocked spit unstated.
9. Larva infobox for completeness: combat 65, 30 HP, max hit 3, Stab, 4 ticks.
10. Sources: Maggot_King, Maggot_King/Strategies, Poisonous_bile, Sticky_bile, Carrion, Ur-maggot_larvae.
