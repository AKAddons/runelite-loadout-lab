# Mood quality ledger

Every compute-animation mood is a work in progress (Andrew, 2026-09-04).
When the renderer (`sprite2text.hybrid`) learns a technique, regenerate
everything with `python3 scripts/art/splice.py all`, eyeball
`python3 scripts/art/bosses.py show`, and refine the pools below the bar
as their own mural issue. Update this ledger with every landing.
